Privacy policy

Sterling Drive Sync only accesses the Google Drive files that it creates itself (the drive.file permission). Its sole purpose is to back up the owner's own files.

  • No data is collected, sold, or shared with anyone.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking.
  • Files move only between the owner's computer and the owner's Google Drive.
  • Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account "Third-party connections" page.
